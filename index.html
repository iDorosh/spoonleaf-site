Is Spoonleaf free?+

Yes. Spoonleaf is being made as a free app for iPhone and iPad. There’s no separate Spoonleaf account to create. The App Store release is coming soon.

Can I import from any recipe website?+

Spoonleaf reads the recipe information many websites already include on their pages. Paste a direct recipe link or share it from Safari, then review everything before saving. Some sites block imports or don’t include readable recipe data, so support varies. The original author and source link stay with your recipe.

How does iCloud work?+

Your cookbook is stored on your device. When iCloud is enabled and available, Spoonleaf uses your private iCloud storage to sync recipes and photos between your devices. It uses your existing Apple account and iCloud storage allowance. You can check availability in Spoonleaf’s Settings; syncing depends on Apple’s services and your device settings.

Can I cook without an internet connection?+

Recipes and photos already saved on your device are available offline. You’ll need a connection to import new recipe links or sync with iCloud. You can also export a cookbook backup from Settings to keep an extra copy.

What happens when I hide a recipe section?+

It disappears from the reading view, but its content stays saved. Open Edit to see hidden sections again, make changes, or bring them back. Your recipe can feel simpler without losing a useful detail.
